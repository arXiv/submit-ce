TEST SUBMISSION: 24_corrupt_archive   (corrupt-archive handling fixture)
============================================================
The artifact IS the corrupt upload: `tarballs/24_corrupt_archive.tar.gz` is a
VALID .tar.gz that has been TRUNCATED mid-stream. There is no real source here.

How to test (upload the tarball on the Upload Files / Prepare Files page):
  BEFORE the fix: the upload fails with the GENERIC "There was a problem
  uploading your file." (the gzip EOFError / TarError falls through to the
  catch-all), and you stay on the Upload page.
  AFTER the fix: you get the SPECIFIC message "We could not read your uploaded
  file. It may be corrupt, incomplete, or not a supported archive format..."
  and stay on the Upload page. No 500 either way.

Mechanism:
  gs_file_store.store_source_package wraps the zip/tgz extraction and translates
  BadZipFile / TarError.ReadError (bad magic / non-archive) AND gzip.BadGzipFile
  (bad CRC) / EOFError (truncated stream) into a ValueError with a friendly
  message. save() re-raises it unchanged; upload_files() catches ValueError and
  flashes the specific message.

Variants to try by hand:
  - garbage bytes named `x.tar.gz` (bad magic)      -> TarError.ReadError
  - this truncated-but-valid-header .tar.gz          -> gzip EOFError
  - a .zip with a corrupt central directory          -> zipfile.BadZipFile
