Editing metadata after submitting:

Once your submission has been announced, amendments to the core metadata may only be made through replacement or withdrawal.

Non-core metadata can be edited at any time after announcement and without a new revision:

  • Journal reference
  • DOI
  • MSC or ACM classification
  • Report number
